# Meme reactions

[← Documentation](README.md)

Make a gesture during a call and one of your pictures pops up over the middle of the video. It covers about two thirds of the frame for 3 seconds, then goes away by itself. Everyone in the call sees it, because it goes out through the virtual webcam like the effects do.

Memes work on top of any effect: while you are visible, while you vanish, and after you are gone. They do not appear while the app is capturing your room.

## Setting them up

Open the **MEMES** tab of the panel.

1. **Choose a picture** for a gesture: click the **+** in its row and pick a file (JPG, PNG, BMP or WEBP). The app copies it into its own folder, so moving or deleting the original does not matter. PNG transparency is kept.
2. Choosing a picture **turns that gesture on**. Use the switch on the right of its row to turn it off and on again. A gesture without a picture cannot be turned on.
3. To **change a picture**, click its thumbnail and pick another file.

The **Memes on** switch at the top turns all of them off and on at once, without forgetting your pictures.

VanishCam comes with **no pictures**: every gesture starts empty, so you choose what shows up in your calls.

## The gestures

Hold the gesture for a moment, about a quarter of a second. To show the same meme again, lower your hands first and make the gesture again: holding it does not repeat it. While a meme is on screen, other gestures do nothing.

| Gesture | How to make it |
| --- | --- |
| **Thumbs up** (*Joinha*) | A fist with only the thumb up, in front of your chest or beside your face. It works even with your other hand in view |
| **Hands behind the head** (*Mãos atrás da cabeça*) | Both hands at the sides of your head, at ear height, as if leaning back |
| **Time-out** (*Tempo*) | The referee's T: one open hand flat and horizontal, the other upright touching it, in front of your chest |
| **Calm down** (*Calma*) | One open hand held out in front of your chest, fingers together and up, palm to the camera |
| **Hands on the face** (*Mãos no rosto*) | Both open hands upright in front of your face, close to each other |
| **Hands together** (*Mãos juntas*) | Palms pressed together or fingers interlaced, anywhere in the frame |
| **Pointing at myself** (*Apontando pra mim*) | Only the index finger out, pointing at your own chest from below your chin |
| **Hand to the ear** (*Mão na orelha*) | One open hand beside your face, at ear height, as if trying to hear better |

When two gestures match at once, the one higher in the table wins.

## Gestures shared with effects

Two gestures are close to an effect's own trigger:

- **Hands together** is the Naruto seal.
- **Hands behind the head** is close to the Ash gesture: both hands raised beside your head.

With that effect selected, the gesture's row on the MEMES tab shows a warning, because one pose could make you vanish and show the meme at the same time. The app does not switch anything off for you: turn the meme off if you do not want both.

## Performance

While at least one meme is on, a second detector looks at your face and hands, next to the effect's own detector. On a slower computer this can make the video less smooth. If it stutters, switch the memes off with **Memes on** on the MEMES tab: the detector stops, and the video goes back to its previous speed.
